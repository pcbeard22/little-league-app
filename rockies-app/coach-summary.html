Lineup Builder

  • Set batting order and field positions for all 6 innings in one view
  • Tap arrows to reorder players (drag on desktop)
  • Field diagram updates in real-time as you assign positions
  • Auto-Suggest generates a recommended lineup using player ratings, depth charts, and AI
  • Save lineups per game and load previous ones from "My Lineups"
  • Game notes section for post-game observations (PIN protected)

Schedule & Standings

  • Live schedule and scores from Game Changer (always current)
  • Full AAA standings — W-L-T, PCT, GB, run differential, streaks

Roster

  • Player cards with positions, stats, and attribute ratings
  • Coach Notes — add your own observations on any player (the app remembers your name)
  • Notes from all coaches feed into the AI lineup suggestions
  • Public-safe by default — sensitive coaching notes are behind a PIN lock

Stats Dashboard

  • Batting leaders: AVG, OBP, OPS, XBH, RBI, SB, QAB%, BA/RISP, SO, LOB
  • Development Focus cards showing who needs work in key areas
  • Tap any stat card to see all 11 players ranked
  • Full sortable batting and pitching tables
  • Team averages: AVG, OBP, OPS, Runs/Game

AI-Powered Lineup Suggestions

  • Auto-Suggest considers: player attributes, depth charts, pitcher rotation, who pitched last game, recent game notes, coach notes, and logic updates
  • Powered by Claude AI, layered on top of a rule-based engine

How to Add Your Player Notes

  1. Go to gamemanager.tech/roster
  2. Tap a player card to expand it
  3. Scroll to Coach Notes
  4. Enter your name the first time — the app remembers you
  5. Type your note and hit "Add Note"

Your notes help the AI make better lineup suggestions for everyone.

Coach Access: Game notes, the Logic page, and sensitive roster info require a PIN. Ask the head coach for the code.

The app works on phone and desktop. Add it to your home screen for quick access.
